## Evidence coverage and gaps

- 12 of 64 indicators have a maturity judgement.
- 6 have at least one evidence reference.
- 3 references are marked reviewed by the assessor.
- 52 indicators are not assessed and remain outside this sample’s evidential coverage.
- High uncertainty remains for A.1.3, A.2.2 and B.2.1.

## Suggested questions to investigate

- **A.1.2 — R-EVIDENCE-GAP:** Which dated operating record shows actual refresh performance over a representative period?
- **A.1.3 — R-HIGH-UNCERTAINTY:** Who can establish the relevant population denominator and explain known coverage bias?
- **A.2.2 — R-HIGH-UNCERTAINTY:** Which team can distinguish technical linkage capability from routinely approved linkage delivery?
- **C–H — R-NOT-ASSESSED:** Which indicators are relevant to the boundary, who should review them and by when?

## Potential improvement actions

These are assessor-entered proposals, not recommendations invented by the tool:

- **A.1.1 — R-USER-ACTION:** Confirm the dataset owner and review current coverage evidence.
- **A.1.3 — R-USER-ACTION:** Agree a documented representativeness measure and review cycle.
- **B.2.1 — R-USER-ACTION:** Compare the stated quality process with two recent delivery records.

## Dependencies and constraints

The data-coverage picture depends on semantic quality, lawful linkage governance and secure infrastructure. The apparent infrastructure strength should not be treated as evidence that staffing headroom or funding is sufficient. Operating capacity is recorded separately from capability and evidence.

## Limitations and responsible use

- This is a partial, self-reported and synthetic example.
- It has not been independently validated, assured or endorsed.
- HDRL’s reliability, validity and fitness for accreditation are not established.
- Results depend on the assessment boundary, participants, interpretation and evidence available on the stated date.
- A maturity judgement does not establish legal compliance, delivery performance, funding eligibility or programme participation.
