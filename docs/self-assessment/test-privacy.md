# Privacy for this test

This public test is provided by OPL Advisory Ltd. It works in your browser without registration, email verification, analytics or an AI service. The planned registered beta is separate and is not enabled here.

## Your draft stays in your browser

The tool saves your assessment boundary, responses, certainty, notes, evidence references and a dated change history in this browser on this device. This supports saving and returning. OPL Advisory cannot retrieve that draft through this test.

There are no evidence uploads, contact forms or automatic feedback submissions. Use invented or non-sensitive information. Do not enter patient information, confidential personal data, credentials or sensitive operational details.

The test does not create beta activity events or collect time spent using it. If you previously used a compatible prototype on this same address, its existing draft and history are preserved; new analytics collection remains off.

## Downloads and deletion

You can download a full backup JSON or summary CSV, print a report, or deliberately prepare a minimised share. A minimised share includes your selected results, declared context and reviewed scope/N/A explanation; it is not a restorable backup. These files may include your assessment text and evidence references. Check their contents before sharing them. A download does not send anything to OPL Advisory or another person.

Use **Delete on-device draft** to remove the saved draft, or clear this site's browser data. Clearing browser data or using another device can make your draft unavailable. Download a full backup JSON before clearing it. Restore reads that file locally and asks before replacing the one saved draft. Cancellation or a failed validation/storage transaction leaves the current draft intact. Restore retains original assessment provenance but excludes contacts, consent, sessions, service settings and prior event history. Downloaded files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them separately.

## Website requests

Your browser requests the page and its files from the website host. The host receives normal request information, such as your IP address and browser details. The assessment does not send your answers, notes, evidence references or report in those requests.

The self-assessment and this privacy page do not load analytics or external fonts. Other pages on the existing framework website may use their own analytics and font services.

## Questions

Use the contact route on the [About and reuse page](../about.md) for questions about the test. Do not include an assessment export or sensitive information unless you deliberately intend to share it. Any copy you send separately is outside the browser-only arrangement described above.
