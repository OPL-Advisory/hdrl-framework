# Framework in Practice

## The 3 Nations Readiness Assessment

The HDRL Framework was developed for an independent assessment of the potential readiness of health data research services in **Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland** to participate in the emerging UK Health Data Research Service (HDRS).

The project was commissioned by **Research Data Scotland** on behalf of the devolved nations and conducted by **OPL Advisory** between December 2025 and April 2026.

## How the framework was applied

The assessment combined five evidence streams:

1. indicator-level readiness assessments using stakeholder interviews and documentary evidence;
2. retrospective and prospective research use-case analysis;
3. a workshop in each nation;
4. a joint 3 Nations synthesis session; and
5. external policy and sector intelligence.

Each nation was offered a structured **Right of Reply** to correct factual inaccuracies and provide additional evidence. Proposed changes were adjudicated against the same maturity descriptors, with cross-nation calibration used to support consistency.

## What the application tested

The field application tested whether one framework could:

- assess heterogeneous systems without requiring a single governance or institutional model;
- distinguish documented maturity from unverified capability;
- identify where policy, capacity, evidence or infrastructure was the binding constraint;
- combine indicator scoring with real research delivery scenarios; and
- produce actionable roadmaps rather than a pass/fail audit.

It also reinforced that an overall score is not enough: domain patterns, evidence gaps, capacity and use-case dependencies all matter to readiness.

## Publication boundary

!!! warning "What this website publishes"
    This website publishes the HDRL Framework and general information about its development and use. It does **not** publish the detailed country-level assessment records, indicator-level scores, submitted evidence or evaluation material. Findings about individual nations should be taken only from the authorised Final Report published by Research Data Scotland.

The website has intentionally not reproduced country-level findings or scores. This keeps the framework reusable while respecting the confidentiality and publication status of the underlying assessments.

## Final Report

Research Data Scotland published the Final Report on **14 July 2026**. It presents the authorised findings from the 3 Nations Readiness Assessment and the roadmap arising from them.

    The public report is the authoritative source for the assessment findings. This website links to it but does not reproduce the underlying indicator-by-country matrix, detailed evidence records or right-of-reply material.
