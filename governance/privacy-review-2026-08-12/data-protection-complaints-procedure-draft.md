## Public routes

People must be able to complain by:

- an accessible electronic form at `[CONFIRM: URL]`;
- email to `privacy@hdrlframework.org`; and
- post to `[CONFIRM: postal address]`.

The public notice must also explain the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

## Intake and acknowledgement

1. Record the received date, route, contact details, issue category and assigned handler in the restricted complaints register. Do not copy assessment content or unnecessary sensitive detail into the register.
2. Distinguish a data-protection complaint from a rights request, security incident, accessibility issue and ordinary product feedback. A message may be more than one and must follow every applicable route.
3. Acknowledge receipt as soon as practical and no later than 30 days after receipt.
4. Provide a reference, handler/contact route, a concise understanding of the complaint and the next expected update. Ask only for information necessary to clarify or verify the matter.

## Investigation

1. Preserve relevant operational records without copying OTPs, assessment content or unrelated personal data into the case file.
2. Identify the processing purpose, lawful basis, systems, suppliers, access history, applicable notice/version and related rights request or incident.
3. Make appropriate enquiries without undue delay. Seek processor evidence through the approved supplier route where necessary.
4. Keep the person informed when the investigation is not concluded promptly. Record the reason and next update date.
5. Escalate a possible personal-data breach immediately to the incident process; the complaints timetable does not replace breach-notification deadlines.

## Outcome

Communicate the outcome without undue delay. The response must:

- address each substantive point;
- explain the evidence considered and the conclusion in plain language;
- state any correction, deletion, restriction, control change or other remedy;
- give the completion date or a realistic implementation date;
- explain how to challenge the outcome internally; and
- explain the right to complain to the ICO.

## Records and oversight

- Restrict the register to the accountable owner and deputy; require MFA and log access.
- Retain complaint records for `[CONFIRM: justified period]`, then delete or retain only a non-identifying learning record.
- Review open cases monthly and completed themes at least quarterly.
- Test deputy cover and the electronic route before public launch.
- Do not use complaint information for marketing, participant scoring, accreditation, funding or participation decisions.

This procedure is based on the ICO's current [data-protection complaints guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/how-to-deal-with-data-protection-complaints/) and requires qualified review if it must operate alongside another statutory or contractual complaints regime.
